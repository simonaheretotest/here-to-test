## What are locators?

Locators allow Playwright to find and interact
with elements on a webpage.

## Why locators matter

Choosing reliable locators helps make automated
tests more maintainable and resilient.

## What I learned

Playwright provides several ways to locate
elements, including roles, text, labels
and placeholders.
